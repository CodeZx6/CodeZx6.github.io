BiST-IF: Enhancing origin–destination flow prediction via bi-directional spatio-temporal inference and interconnected feature evolution

Piao Yu, Xu Zhang, Yongshun Gong, Jian Zhang, Haoliang Sun, Junjie Zhang, Xinxin Zhang, Yilong Yin

Expert Systems with Applications, vol. 264, 125679 (2025)

Also indexed in: Semantic Scholar · OpenAlex · dblp

TL;DR. BiST-IF predicts metro and taxi origin-destination flows by correcting delayed OD matrices, applying bi-directional origin/destination attention, and letting OD and arrival (Out-OD) flows evolve jointly through mutual-information attention.

Key points

  • OD Delay Correction estimates delayed OD distributions from periodic patterns and recent Out-OD flows.
  • OD Bi-directional Attention extracts trends with BiLSTM and origin-wise plus destination-wise attention.
  • Mutual Information Flow Evolution fuses OD and Out-OD features with multi-head attention and gated updates.
  • Evaluated on HZMetro (80 stations, 10-minute intervals) and NYC-TOD2018 (69 Manhattan taxi zones, 30-minute intervals).

Abstract

Origin–destination (OD) flow prediction is crucial for predicting inter-station passenger flows in intelligent transport systems. However, previous OD prediction methods have ignored the delay of OD flows and failed to focus on the supplementary effect of the arrival OD flow (Out-OD) flow data on OD flows. We innovatively propose an OD flow prediction method based on Bidirectional Attention and Interconnected Feature Evolution (BiST-IF) to address these challenges. Firstly, we propose a correction method based on period delay probability and real-time flow features for OD flow information. Furthermore, for the flow information of different periods, we design a bi-directional attention module to achieve the preliminary prediction by deconstructing and analyzing the temporal and flow features of OD flow and fusing the temporal and spatial features. After that, we design the mutual information module of Out-OD and OD flows to model their interconnected feature evolution.

Frequently asked questions

What is the OD delay problem?

A trip's destination is only known when it ends, so recent OD matrices are incomplete. BiST-IF corrects them using period-delay probability and real-time flow features.

What is Out-OD flow and why use it?

Out-OD is the arrival-side OD flow. BiST-IF models the mutual information between OD and Out-OD flows so the two evolve jointly, improving destination-aware inference.

Which datasets were used?

HZMetro (Hangzhou metro, 80 stations, 10-minute intervals) and NYC-TOD2018 (69 Manhattan taxi zones, 30-minute intervals).

中文摘要

通过双向时空推理与互联特征演化增强起讫点（OD）流量预测（BiST-IF）

OD 流量预测对智能交通系统中的站间客流预测至关重要。以往方法忽略了 OD 流的延迟，也未利用到达 OD 流（Out-OD）对 OD 流的补充作用。BiST-IF 首先基于周期延迟概率与实时流量特征对 OD 流信息进行校正；其次针对不同周期的流量信息设计双向注意力模块，分解分析 OD 流的时间与流量特征并融合时空特征得到初步预测；随后设计 Out-OD 与 OD 流的互信息模块，建模二者互联的特征演化。在 HZMetro 与 NYC-TOD2018 上评测。

关键词：OD 流量预测；起讫点矩阵；OD 延迟校正；双向注意力；互信息；地铁客流；出租车 OD；智能交通
