Abstract

Preprocessing procedures are commonly employed to reduce water-absorption bands and noise in hyperspectral images (HSIs). Nevertheless, they typically do not entirely eradicate noise. This is especially evident in scenarios that necessitate data of exceptional quality, such as cross-domain few-shot classification tasks. Within these specific conditions, the influence of remaining background noise on the ultimate results of classification is substantial. Furthermore, the presence of sample selection biases in the few-shot task might lead to the emergence of false statistical correlations between data from distinct domains, resulting in a decrease in the model’s ability to generalize. We propose a new method called spatial-spectral–semantic cross-domain few-shot learning (S3CFSL) to address the challenge. This method promotes the learning of transferable information by incorporating feature denoising operations in the feature extraction process to restore essential information. Concurrently, it enhances cross-domain distributional consistency by introducing a semantic-aware strategy to strengthen the association between cross-domain data and semantic information. Specifically, the spatial and spectral dual channels (SSDCs), in conjunction with the cross-spatial-spectral transformer (CSST), are designed as a feature extractor to acquire interactive spatial-spectral features. The feature-denoising operations can further acquiring transferable information from cross-domain features, thus facilitating meta-learning in both the source domain (SD) and the target domain (TD). Meanwhile, a semantic-enhanced domain alignment (SEDA) is designed to promote domain adaptation by using a semantic-aware strategy, which significantly enhances distributional consistency for cross-domain tasks. Our results exhibit exceptional classification efficacy in comparison to other state-of-the-art approaches on three public HSI datasets.

Frequently asked questions

What is cross-domain few-shot HSI classification?

Learning to classify a new hyperspectral scene (target domain) from very few labelled samples by meta-learning on a different, labelled source domain.

How does S3CFSL improve transfer?

Feature-denoising operations restore essential information for transferable features, and a semantic-aware domain alignment (SEDA) strengthens the link between cross-domain data and semantic information, improving distributional consistency.

中文摘要

面向高光谱图像分类的空间-光谱-语义跨域小样本学习（S3CFSL）

高光谱图像预处理通常无法完全消除噪声，在跨域小样本分类等对数据质量要求极高的场景中，残余背景噪声显著影响结果；小样本任务中的样本选择偏差还会导致跨域数据间的虚假统计相关，降低泛化能力。S3CFSL 在特征提取中引入特征去噪以恢复关键信息、促进可迁移信息学习，并通过语义感知策略增强跨域数据与语义信息的关联。具体地，空间与光谱双通道 SSDC 与跨空间-光谱 Transformer CSST 构成特征提取器，特征去噪促进源域与目标域上的元学习，语义增强域对齐 SEDA 提升跨域分布一致性。在三个公开高光谱数据集上优于现有方法。

关键词：高光谱图像分类；跨域小样本学习；特征去噪；语义域对齐；元学习；Transformer；遥感；IEEE TGRS
