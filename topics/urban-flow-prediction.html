Urban flow prediction with masked and contrastive spatio-temporal pre-training

Urban flow prediction forecasts future flows across city regions from historical flow data and supports services such as trip planning, congestion control, and public safety. Three papers (Zhang et al., CIKM 2023; Zhang et al., Knowledge-Based Systems 2023; Zhang et al., Neural Networks 2025) develop self-supervised spatio-temporal pre-training for this task.

MC-STL (CIKM 2023; code repository MCSTL) combines a mask-enhanced pre-training task with contrastive pre-training and inter-regional attention to capture regional correlations that change over time. ST-FCL (Knowledge-Based Systems 2023; code repository ST-CSL) applies contrastive learning separately in a temporal view and a spatial view to capture global periodicity and the hidden relations between functionally similar regions. MR-UFP (Neural Networks 2025) pairs spatial-temporal random masking and contrastive pre-training with a multi-scale region-classification auxiliary task, and is designed to remain robust on small or noisy datasets.

MR-UFP: Enhancing urban flow prediction via mutual reinforcement with multi-scale regional information

Xu Zhang, Mengxin Cao, Yongshun Gong, Xiaoming Wu, Xiangjun Dong, Ying Guo, Long Zhao, Chengqi Zhang · Neural Networks, vol. 182, 106900 (2025)

MR-UFP improves urban flow prediction on small or noisy data by pre-training with spatial-temporal masking and contrastive learning, then jointly training the predictor with a multi-scale region-classification task that mutually reinforces spatial features.

  • Spatial-temporal random masking plus contrastive pre-training mines heterogeneity directly from historical flows.
  • Spatial feature extraction is reformulated as a mutual-reinforcement task with a multi-scale regional classification auxiliary task, with adaptive environment fusion and real-time graph processing.
  • A joint loss balances the two tasks; experiments on two real-world datasets show gains over baselines even with minimal data.

ST-CSL: Spatio-temporal fusion and contrastive learning for urban flow prediction

Xu Zhang, Yongshun Gong, Chengqi Zhang, Xiaoming Wu, Ying Guo, Wenpeng Lu, Long Zhao, Xiangjun Dong · Knowledge-Based Systems, vol. 282, 111104 (2023)

ST-CSL fuses temporal-view and spatial-view contrastive learning so an urban flow predictor captures global periodicity and the hidden flow relationships between functionally similar regions.

  • Temporal-view extraction captures the distribution and change of global urban-flow periodicity.
  • Spatial-view extraction captures implied flow variation between similar regions.
  • Separate closeness, period, and trend encoders are fused by a residual fusion network.

MCSTL: Mask- and Contrast-Enhanced Spatio-Temporal Learning for Urban Flow Prediction

Xu Zhang, Yongshun Gong, Xinxin Zhang, Xiaoming Wu, Chengqi Zhang, Xiangjun Dong · CIKM 2023, pp. 3298–3307

MCSTL pre-trains an urban flow model with a mask-reconstruction task across space and time and a graph-based contrastive task that weights regions by inter-regional attention, then fine-tunes for flow prediction.

  • A mask-enhanced pre-training task learns latent correlations across spatial and temporal dimensions.
  • A graph-based method uses inter-regional attention weights to derive contrastive correlations between regions.
  • Published open access in the ACM Digital Library (CIKM 2023, pages 3298-3307).

Frequently asked questions

Which papers use masked pre-training for urban flow prediction?

MC-STL (CIKM 2023) introduces a mask-enhanced pre-training task across the spatial and temporal dimensions, and MR-UFP (Neural Networks 2025) uses spatial-temporal random masking together with spatial-temporal contrastive pre-training.

How do MC-STL, ST-FCL and MR-UFP differ?

MC-STL pre-trains two encoders with mask reconstruction and a contrastive task with global-local cross-attention. ST-FCL builds separate temporal-view and spatial-view contrastive modules. MR-UFP adds a multi-scale region-classification auxiliary task that mutually reinforces spatial feature learning, balanced by a joint loss.

基于掩码与对比预训练的城市流量预测

城市流量预测根据历史流量预测城市各区域未来的流量，服务于出行规划、拥堵控制与公共安全。三篇论文围绕自监督时空预训练展开：MC-STL（CIKM 2023）结合掩码增强预训练与对比预训练；ST-FCL（Knowledge-Based Systems 2023）在时间视图与空间视图上分别做对比学习；MR-UFP（Neural Networks 2025）将时空随机掩码、对比预训练与多尺度区域分类辅助任务结合，在小数据或噪声数据上依然稳健。
